At VoltCore, safety is not a checklist — it is our operating system. Every technician undergoes rigorous OSHA-certified training before touching a single wire. We lock out, tag out, and verify every circuit before work begins. Our zero-accident record is not luck. It is a culture we have built over 12 years and thousands of projects.
